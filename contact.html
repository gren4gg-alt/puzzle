Contact Broshere

Broshere is maintained by one person, so replies may take some time. Every message is read.

Feedback and suggestions

You can contact Broshere if you have feedback about a tool, a suggestion for a new web utility or mini-game, or an idea that could improve the site.

Bug reports

If a tool is not working correctly, please include the name of the tool, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Screenshots or browser/device details can also be helpful when relevant.

Help us reproduce a problem

  1. Send the page name or link and your browser and device.
  2. Describe the steps immediately before the problem.
  3. Include the exact error message and what you expected instead.
  4. For a game, mention the level or mode. For a file tool, mention the format and approximate size.

A small non-sensitive sample is helpful only if the problem depends on a particular file. Please do not email identity documents, private photos, signatures, passwords, or other sensitive material just to demonstrate a bug.

Guide corrections

If a control, rule, or output differs from a guide, include the guide’s title and the sentence that seems wrong. Suggestions for an additional worked example are welcome too.
