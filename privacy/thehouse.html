Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026
Android package: com.broshere.thehouse

Broshere provides The House, a horror game for teens aged 13 and older and adults. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, use multiplayer or voice chat, view ads, or contact us.

At a glance

Solo play does not require an account. Progress and settings are saved on your device. Multiplayer exchanges connection and game-session information. Voice chat is optional. The Android app uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads, which processes additional device and advertising information.

1. Information the game handles

  • On-device information. Settings, control layouts, character unlocks, shards and reward receipts are stored locally to keep your preferences and progress. Current-run checkpoints are not a permanent cloud save. We do not upload these local records to a Broshere save server in this version.
  • Multiplayer information. When you host or join a room, the game exchanges your chosen display name, temporary peer/session identifiers, room information and gameplay state with the host and other participants as needed. Shared drawings or other content you create in a room are visible to its participants.
  • Connection information. Multiplayer uses PeerJS signaling and WebRTC connections, with STUN or relay services when available. These services process network information such as IP addresses and connection identifiers. Direct peer connections can expose your IP address to other participants.
  • Advertising information. The Android advertising SDK processes information described in the advertising section below.
  • Support information. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots, logs or other information you choose to attach.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, photo library, SMS, call history or precise device location. Fictional medicine and injuries in the game do not involve collecting health information.

2. Optional microphone and voice chat

Microphone permission is used for live multiplayer voice chat. You can play without granting it. When you enable voice chat and grant permission, the game captures microphone audio; transmission follows your mute and push-to-talk settings. Audio levels are also processed on your device for speaking indicators and noise-related gameplay.

This version sends voice through WebRTC peer connections. Broshere does not record or keep a voice-chat archive. Other participants can hear transmitted speech and may independently record it, which we cannot prevent. Do not share personal or sensitive information in a room.

Disable voice chat or revoke microphone permission in your device settings to stop microphone access. Muting and push-to-talk control transmission; they should not be treated as revoking microphone permission.

3. Rewarded ads and Google AdMob

The Android app offers rewarded ads for optional in-game rewards. Ad availability is not guaranteed. Advertising services may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising and other device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Google handles advertising data under its policies. Where required, the app presents Google's consent flow before requesting ads. You can access available ad privacy choices from How to play. Android also provides controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. Consent choices and platform settings can affect the ads and information processed.

The website currently uses a local ad demonstration instead of AdMob. Google test ads may be used in development and testing builds; test mode should not be taken to mean that no SDK data is processed.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

4. How information is used and disclosed

Information is used to operate the game, connect room participants, deliver voice chat and rewards, remember preferences, respond to support requests, and support advertising and fraud prevention. Multiplayer services and advertising providers receive the information necessary for their features. Room participants receive information and content needed to play together.

We do not operate a business selling player profiles. Advertising SDK disclosures are described above; this statement does not mean that no information is shared with advertising providers. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or reasonably necessary to address abuse, protect users or defend legal rights.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their retention practices and international safeguards are described in their respective policies. This version does not use Epic Online Services or Google Play Integrity; this policy will be updated before an integration changes the game's data practices.

5. Retention and security

Local saves remain until you clear the game's storage, remove the relevant browser data or otherwise delete them. Device backup or restore features may preserve app data according to your platform settings. Clearing local data removes settings, shards and unlocks and cannot be undone by Broshere.

Multiplayer state and live audio are processed for the session; Broshere does not maintain a central history of matches, shared drawings or voice recordings in this version. This does not guarantee that network providers keep no connection logs or that other participants keep no copies.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to respond to your request and meet applicable legal or security needs. Advertising and network providers determine retention for information they process under their own practices.

WebRTC protects game and voice traffic in transit, and Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data uses TLS encryption. We keep privileged service credentials out of public game files. No transmission method or device is completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

  • Play solo to avoid joining multiplayer rooms.
  • Use a display name that does not identify you, and share room codes only with intended participants.
  • Disable voice, mute participants, or revoke microphone permission.
  • Use available in-app ad privacy choices and Android advertising controls.
  • Clear app storage or browser site data to delete local progress and preferences.
  • Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information Broshere holds about you, or to raise a privacy concern. Rights and applicable exceptions depend on your location.

This version does not offer a Broshere account-registration system. We cannot remotely recover or delete a local save on your device, or erase copies independently retained by other players. For information controlled independently by Google or another provider, its own privacy controls may also be necessary.

7. Age suitability

The House is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Regional store ratings may require a higher age because of the game's horror and violence. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Broshere, contact us so we can investigate and address information within our control.
