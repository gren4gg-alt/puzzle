DesertRunner Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 8 October 2026

Broshere provides DesertRunner (Android package com.myname.mygame). This policy explains how information is handled in this game. It does not automatically cover other Broshere games or all activity on our website; those services may have separate privacy policies.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your scores, distance records, shards, unlocked costumes, selected costume, settings, control preferences and tutorial progress locally on your device. No game account or sign-in is required. The current game does not upload these local game records to a Broshere game server.

When you choose to use rewarded ads, the game may ask for an age range. You can select “Prefer not to say.” This preference is saved locally; the game does not ask for your date of birth or send your selected age range to a Broshere server.

2. Optional rewarded advertisements

The Android game uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads, such as earning shards or continuing a run. Choosing not to watch an ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay. A reward is granted only when the ad service reports that the reward was earned.

Google and its advertising services may process information needed to provide ads, measure ad interactions, diagnose problems and prevent fraud. Depending on the applicable settings and restrictions, this can include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and app information, permitted identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable advertising privacy choices and consent information. Where available, the game’s Settings include an “Ad privacy” control. Device or service settings may provide additional privacy choices.

Learn more from Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Children and family audiences

DesertRunner is intended for a mixed audience, including children. The current Android release applies child-directed advertising treatment and a general-audience maximum ad-content rating to all ad requests. It also sets the consent service’s under-age flag. The game does not request personalized, interest-based advertising through an adult advertising path.

These safeguards do not mean the ad service processes no data. Processing remains subject to the service’s applicable child-directed restrictions. The game does not request a child’s name, contact details or exact birth date during gameplay. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact us at the address below.

4. Controls, permissions and external services

If you enable tilt steering, device-orientation readings are used locally to control movement. The game does not upload those readings to Broshere. The current game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access for gameplay.

The game may download interface fonts from Google Fonts. Requests to that service can disclose connection information such as IP address and browser or device details. Advertising and external links also require network access. External services handle information under their own policies.

5. Contacting us and sharing information

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request, provide support and resolve related issues. Avoid sending sensitive information or a child’s personal information unless necessary for your request.

Broshere does not sell your local game records or support correspondence. Service providers may process support correspondence to deliver and store email. Advertising-related processing is described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address security or protect rights. Third-party services may process information in countries other than your own.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Local game records remain until you clear the app’s storage, remove the relevant browser site data or uninstall the game, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. Removing this data can permanently erase your progress and unlocked items.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of information you have provided to Broshere. Google controls retention and deletion for data it processes; see its privacy policy and privacy controls.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify it before responding.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. We will publish the revised policy here with an updated date and provide additional notice where required.
