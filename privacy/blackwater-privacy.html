Brohere / BLACKWATER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

BLACKWATER is a horror puzzle game provided by Brohere. This policy explains the information used by the game, its optional rewarded advertisements, and our support service. Contact us at broshere@broshere.com.

1. Playing the game

You can play BLACKWATER without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

Progress, completed puzzles, collected objects, notebook entries, settings, control layouts, and rewarded-hint records are stored locally on your device. The game does not upload these save files to a Brohere server. A random local run identifier helps prevent a completed ad reward from being applied twice; the game does not send that identifier to our advertising provider.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

The Android game integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may choose to watch a rewarded ad for an additional puzzle hint or to restore the friendly Warden. Ads are not required to resume after death. Choosing not to watch an ad does not remove your saved progress.

When permitted by the applicable privacy choices, the game initializes advertising and may preload an ad before you choose to watch it. Information can therefore be processed when an ad is requested, even if you do not watch that ad.

Depending on device settings, consent, region, and the ad served, Google's advertising services may collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views;
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as launch time, crashes or hangs, and energy usage;
  • Privacy-choice and consent information needed to apply your advertising preferences.

Google uses this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics associated with its advertising services, security, and fraud prevention. Its SDK may store information on your device. Google states that the user data its Mobile Ads SDK collects is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy, information about partner apps and sites, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Brohere does not operate a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service in this version. This does not prevent the advertising SDK from processing the diagnostics described above.

3. Your advertising and privacy choices

Where required, Google presents a privacy message so you can make advertising choices. The game checks UMP's permission to request ads before requesting them. If that permission is unavailable, rewarded advertising stays unavailable and ordinary gameplay remains accessible.

When Google requires an ongoing privacy entry point, AD PRIVACY CHOICES is available in the game's main and pause menus. Use it to review or change your choices. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be limited or non-personalized; these ads can still use information for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

You can also use Android's advertising and privacy settings to reset or delete your advertising ID, where supported. You can decline any offer to watch a rewarded ad.

4. Contacting support

If you email broshere@broshere.com, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you send. We use that information to respond, investigate the problem, and maintain relevant support records. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Sharing and international processing

Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising-service partners under their policies and your applicable choices. Email providers process support messages as needed to deliver and store them. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect users and the service. Service providers may process information outside your country.

6. Retention, deletion, and other rights

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup behaviour. Starting a new run replaces that run's progress; clearing app storage removes local settings and saved records. Removing local game data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.

Google determines retention and deletion for information held by its advertising services. Review Google's policy and account/device privacy controls for those choices. We keep support messages for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Contact us for requests concerning information Brohere holds. We do not have an account database through which to identify or retrieve a player's locally stored progress or Google's device identifiers.

7. Children

BLACKWATER contains horror themes and is not designed for children. Follow the game's age rating in your region. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and address it.

8. Security and changes

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. If the game or its data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek a new privacy choice.
