Privacy & Advertising Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026
Android package: con.broshere.tilttrails

Broshere provides Tilt Trails, a single-player maze game for a general audience that includes children under 13. This policy explains the information handled when you play, choose controls and themes, watch an optional ad, or contact us. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us with questions.

At a glance

No account is required. Progress, settings and cosmetic unlocks stay on your device. Motion readings control the balls locally. Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob, configured for child-directed, non-personalized requests for every player. The game has no multiplayer, chat, microphone recording or Broshere cloud-save service.

1. Information the game handles

  • Local game records. Level progress, earned stars, lives and recovery times, temporary time-boost expiry, tutorial completion, unlocked themes and ball designs, selected theme and ball, control preferences, sound choices, graphics settings and your broad age-group choice are stored on your device. Broshere does not upload these records to a player database in this version.
  • Motion controls. Tilt mode reads device motion sensors to steer the balls and calibrate a comfortable position. These readings are processed locally and are not saved as a motion history or sent by the game to Broshere. Touch mode disables the game's sensor-based steering.
  • Advertising information. Google processes information needed for the advertising service, described below.
  • Support messages. If a parent, guardian or player emails us, we receive the sender's email address, message and anything they choose to attach. Please do not include sensitive information or unnecessary information about a child.

The game does not ask for contacts, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, call history or precise-location permission. It does not ask players for their name, date of birth, email address or an account login. Before advertising starts on Android, a neutral choice offers “12 or younger”, “13 or older” and “Prefer not to say”. Only that broad choice is saved locally; it is not sent to the advertising SDK or Broshere. Opening this policy in a browser contacts the website host, which may process ordinary connection logs under that host's practices.

2. Optional rewarded ads

Players can choose to watch a rewarded ad for lives, a temporary time boost, a cosmetic theme or either of two exclusive marble designs. The offer identifies the reward before the ad starts. All 140 maze layouts remain available through normal progression. Eight other collectible marbles are earned by completing levels, and a polished steel starter is always available. Each advertised cosmetic offer requires one completed ad for that item; the unlock is retained locally until app data is removed. Ball designs do not change movement or physics. No cash purchase or transferable reward is offered.

A reward is granted only after the SDK confirms it was earned and the ad closes. An unavailable ad, a loading/display error, or closing without earning the reward does not grant an unlock. Ad availability is controlled by the provider. Development/debug exports use Google's demo rewarded unit; release exports use Broshere's rewarded unit.

After the age-group choice and advertising privacy checks, the SDK may initialize and preload an ad before you choose to watch one. Choosing not to watch an ad does not prevent all advertising-service network traffic.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK can process and share IP addresses, product/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address can indicate approximate location. This app configures restricted requests as described in the children's section. That does not mean no network or diagnostic information is processed. Google controls retention of information it receives under its own policies.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to refresh privacy information and gate ad requests. Under-age treatment is applied to every player, so the app does not ask children to consent to personalized ads. If Google reports that a privacy-options entry point is required, an Ad privacy choices action appears in Settings. A failed privacy check that does not permit ads leaves rewarded ads unavailable; ordinary gameplay remains usable.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, information about partner apps, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. This policy page contains no advertising, tracking scripts, embedded media or external fonts.

3. Children and family play

Tilt Trails includes children under 13 in its audience. The age-group screen does not request an exact age or date of birth. We apply the same conservative advertising configuration to every player, including older players and those who prefer not to answer: child-directed treatment, under-age-of-consent treatment, non-personalized ad requests and a maximum G ad-content rating. Selecting an older age group never enables personalized ads. The Android manifest excludes advertising-ID and Android advertising-services permissions. These settings restrict advertising behavior; they are not a promise that third-party services receive no information or that every ad is appropriate for every child.

The game has no public profiles, user-generated content, chat or social sharing system. It does not invite children to submit personal information to Broshere. A parent or guardian can contact us to review, correct or request deletion of information we hold about their child. We may ask for reasonable verification of parental authority. If we learn that personal information was provided by a child inappropriately, we will address information within our control.

4. Uses, sharing and retention

Local records operate the game and remember progress and preferences. Support correspondence is used to respond to requests and address security or legal needs. We do not operate a business selling player profiles. Google receives advertising-related information for its service as explained above; the absence of a Broshere account does not make ads anonymous.

Local records remain until you clear app storage or otherwise remove them. Theme and ball unlocks are not backed by a Broshere account and cannot be restored by us after local data is deleted. This Android configuration disables the app's standard backup feature. Operating-system, device-transfer and third-party backup behavior may vary.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Service providers may process information in other countries under their own retention and protection practices. We may disclose information we hold when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users, address abuse or defend legal rights. No device or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Choose Touch instead of Tilt in Settings.
  • Choose whether to start a rewarded ad; cosmetic themes do not change level layouts.
  • Use available Ad privacy choices in Settings and applicable Android privacy controls.
  • Review or change your locally stored age-group choice through Settings → Age group on Android. All choices retain the same restricted advertising configuration.
  • Clear app storage to delete local progress, preferences, boost records, theme unlocks and ball unlocks. This cannot be undone by Broshere.
  • Contact us about access, correction, deletion or another privacy concern involving information Broshere holds. Available rights and exceptions depend on your location.

We cannot remotely access or delete a local save on your phone. For information Google controls independently, its privacy controls or a request to Google may also be needed.
