Privacy & Advertising Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Android package: com.broshere.thegrampshouse

Broshere provides The Gramp's House, a survival horror game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play, use multiplayer or voice chat, view ads, or contact us.

At a glance

Game preferences and single-player progress are stored on your device. Online co-op uses Epic Online Services to connect players. Voice chat is optional. Google AdMob processes information to provide and measure ads.

1. Information the game handles

  • On-device information. The game stores settings, control preferences, character progress and single-player save data on your device. Multiplayer gameplay is not saved as a single-player checkpoint.
  • Multiplayer and connection information. When you create or join a private room, the game and Epic Online Services process information needed to identify and connect participants, including device or product user identifiers, room and session information, IP address and gameplay state. Room participants receive the information needed to play together.
  • Microphone audio. If you choose to enable voice chat and grant microphone permission, live audio is captured and sent through Epic Online Services to the other participant. See the voice section below.
  • Advertising information. Google Mobile Ads processes identifiers, network information, interactions and diagnostics as described below.
  • Support information. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots, logs or other information you choose to send.

The game does not ask for precise-location permission. IP addresses may be used by service providers to estimate a general location.

2. Optional microphone and voice chat

Microphone access is used for multiplayer voice chat. You can play without enabling your microphone. The microphone is off by default; when you turn it on and grant the required permission, the game captures audio and sends it to Epic's voice service for delivery to the other player. Turning the microphone off stops the game's microphone capture. You can also revoke its permission in Android settings.

Other participants can hear what you send and may independently record it. Epic processes voice and connection data to provide its service; its own privacy practices and retention periods apply. Do not share sensitive information in voice chat. See the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including rewarded and interstitial ads. You can choose whether to watch an offered rewarded ad. Ad availability and delivery depend on the advertising service.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including:

  • Network information and approximate location: IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device or account identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers.
  • Interactions: information such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics: app and SDK performance information such as launch time, hangs and energy use.

Where available, the game uses Google's privacy-choice tools to request or manage advertising consent. The options shown depend on your location and the ad service. Android also provides controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. These choices do not necessarily stop all information needed to serve ads or prevent fraud.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Advertising partners configured through Google may have their own practices.

4. How information is used and shared

Information is used to run the game, save local preferences and progress, connect players, provide optional voice chat, serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and answer support requests. Epic Online Services and Google receive information needed to provide their respective services. Your co-op partner receives the gameplay and voice information needed for the session.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies explain their own processing and international data practices.

5. Retention, deletion and security

Local settings and saves remain on your device until you clear the game's app storage or uninstall it, subject to any device backup or restore settings. Clearing app data can remove progress and settings permanently. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your device or copies independently retained by another player.

Epic and Google determine retention for information they process under their own policies and the services configured for the game. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to respond and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You can contact us about access, correction or deletion of information we hold.

We use the security features provided by the game platform and service providers. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

6. Your choices

  • Play solo if you do not want to join a multiplayer room.
  • Leave the microphone off or revoke microphone permission in Android settings.
  • Use any available in-game advertising privacy choices and Android advertising controls.
  • Clear app storage to remove local game data, noting that this also removes local progress.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning information we hold. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information.

For data independently controlled by Epic or Google, you may also need to use their privacy controls or contact them directly.
