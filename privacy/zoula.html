Privacy & Advertising Policy

Updated 21 November 2026

Broshere provides ZOULA, a co-op horror escape-room game. This policy explains how the game and its service providers handle information when you play, use voice chat, view ads, or contact us.

At a glance

Your settings are saved on your device. The host keeps the saved run. Epic Online Services connects the two players. On Android, voice chat is optional and restricted to players whose Google Play age signal indicates they are 18 or older. The Google Play release uses AdMob with age-protective settings and requests non-personalized ads.

1. Game and multiplayer information

  • Settings and saved runs. Your settings, including control layout and audio preferences, are stored on your device. A named co-op run is saved on the host’s device and may include both players’ positions, items and puzzle progress so the host can resume it.
  • Guest sign-in and rooms. Epic Online Services uses a device-linked guest credential to sign you in for multiplayer. Epic receives device or platform information used to create the credential and assigns a game-service identifier. The game and Epic exchange information needed to create or join a room, including display names, room codes and connection identifiers.
  • Gameplay and connections. Connected players exchange game state, such as movement, items and puzzle progress. Networking uses a direct connection where possible or an Epic relay. Epic processes network information, such as IP addresses, to provide the connection. A direct connection may expose your IP address to your partner.
  • Support. If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments you choose to send.

Read the Epic Games Privacy Policy for information about Epic’s processing.

2. Microphone and live voice chat

During a connected session, grant the operating system’s microphone permission and turn on the in-game microphone to speak. Switching the microphone off stops game microphone capture and transmission. You can still hear your partner when eligible voice chat is available. You can also revoke the system permission in your device settings.

Live speech is sent to your partner using Epic voice services or the game’s peer connection. The game processes speech activity for the speaking indicator. Broshere keeps no voice recording archive. People who hear you may independently record what they hear.

3. Google Play age checks for voice

On Android, ZOULA uses the Play Age Signals API to check eligibility for voice chat. Google Play may return an age band or related sharing, verification or approval status. The game keeps a voice eligibility status in memory. It does not collect your date of birth or put the age signal into saved runs or multiplayer messages.

Google Play may ask you to share or verify your age. If the app cannot confirm that you are at least 18, voice chat stays off and you can still play. ZOULA uses the signal exclusively for voice eligibility and never sends it to AdMob or uses it for advertising, analytics or profiling. See Google’s Play Age Signals documentation.

4. Google AdMob and privacy choices

The Google Play release uses AdMob for optional rewarded ads that can revive your character and for occasional interstitial ads when you respawn. A rewarded ad grants a revive when the SDK confirms you earned the reward and the ad closes. If the rewarded ad is unavailable, the game shows a message. Respawning continues when an interstitial is unavailable.

The game requests non-personalized ads, sets a G maximum ad content rating and uses child-directed request settings. The release excludes access to the Android advertising ID through the Mobile Ads SDK’s advertising ID permission. Depending on its configuration, the SDK may still handle IP addresses or approximate location derived from them, app interactions and ad views, diagnostics and applicable device or app identifiers. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

The app asks Google’s User Messaging Platform to check privacy requirements before making ad requests. When available, you can reopen Google’s privacy controls through Settings → Audio → Ad privacy choices. You can skip any optional rewarded ad.

Read the Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

5. Sharing, retention and security

Broshere uses this information to operate the game, connect players, remember progress and preferences, support advertising, and respond to support requests. Your partner receives the information needed for co-op play and any speech you transmit. Epic and Google process information for their services. Their policies explain their retention practices and how information may be processed across borders.

Named runs remain on the host’s device until the host deletes them through Saved Tracks or clears the game’s storage. Local preferences remain until the player resets them or clears app storage. Device backups may preserve local app data under your platform settings. Broshere does not maintain a cloud store for named runs. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal, security and recordkeeping needs.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its data in transit. Broshere may disclose information it holds when legally required or where reasonably necessary to protect players or address abuse.

6. Your choices and requests

  • Choose a display name that does not identify you and share room codes with the people you want to play with.
  • Turn off the microphone or revoke its permission whenever you choose.
  • Use the Google Play and in-game advertising privacy controls when they are offered.
  • Delete a hosted run from Saved Tracks or clear the app’s storage to remove local game data. Ask the room host to delete a run stored on their device.
  • Contact Broshere to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, or to report a privacy concern. Available rights vary by location.

ZOULA does not ask you to create a Broshere account. For data held independently by a service provider, use that provider’s privacy channels. If you believe a child has shared personal information with Broshere, email us so we can investigate and address information within our control.
