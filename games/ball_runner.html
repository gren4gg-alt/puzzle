HOW TO PLAY

Roll forever and dodge whatever the world throws at you. Distance is your score — the longer you survive, the higher it climbs.

Keyboard:
W/Space/↑ Jump (tap twice to double-jump) · S/↓ Duck · AD/←→ Dodge left/right

Touch: ◀▶ to move, ▲ to jump (double-tap for double-jump), ▼ to duck.

Grab the 🛡️ shield pickups to survive one extra hit. Watch for the red warning flashes — they signal incoming spears or obstacles. Different biomes bring different hazards, so stay alert as the world changes.
