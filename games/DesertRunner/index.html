How to play

DUNE RUNNER

↔
Steer across the valley
A and D, or the arrow keys. Drag anywhere on the screen, or switch on gyro steering in the gear menu and tilt the phone — Tilt for tilt alone, Tilt+Swipe to keep the sideways flick dodge as well. Sensitivity for both lives in the same menu. You run forward on your own — you only choose the line.
▲
Jump the logs
W or Space. Swipe up. A log lies right across the valley. There is no way round one — jumping is the only answer.
◉
Read the storm
Sometimes the valley clears and the sky opens. A red ring burns onto the sand where a rock is about to land, and it is aimed at the line you are on — so standing still is always wrong. Move off the ring early. It is the warning; by the time you can see the rock falling, it has already chosen its spot.
▼
Get back down
Hold S or the down arrow to brake. You speed up the further you run, and braking buys time to read a wall and pick your line — it costs distance, not the chain. Swipe down (or S) in mid-air to slam straight back to the sand. A jump you started too early is otherwise a long time to spend in the air.
■
Find the gap in the walls
Stone and block walls close the valley except for one opening. They are too tall to jump, so pick your line early and steer to it.
●
Dodge the rolling boulders
They cross the valley and turn at the walls. Do not jump them — going over the top sets them off and the blast catches you. Go around.
◆
Collect shards, keep the chain
Shards follow the safe line through each obstacle. Every one extends your chain and raises the multiplier; a hit ends it. Squeezing past an obstacle scores too.
⚙
Tune it
The gear icon has quality and steering sensitivity. M mutes, Esc pauses.
