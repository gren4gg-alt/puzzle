How to play Cozy Fit

Fill the pale puzzle silhouette with all the colorful pieces parked around it. Every piece must stay inside the playable shape, and pieces cannot overlap. The board is complete when all its active cells are filled. Holes and cutouts belong to the silhouette, so leave them empty.

Start with the trail

Choose Continue to open the highest level you have unlocked, or select a completed level to replay it. The 50-level trail begins with large square blocks, then introduces mixed pieces, rotation, narrow corridors, and shapes with internal gaps. Completing a level opens the next stop.

Drag, select, and rotate

Drag a piece toward the board and follow the snapping preview. Tap a piece to select it; tap again to deselect it. Rotation becomes available in level 3: select a piece, finish any drag, and press Rotate or R. To undo a placement, pick the piece up again. Drag it outside until DROP TO REMOVE appears to return it to its parking spot.

Read the remaining space

A piece can fit legally and still make the rest of the board impossible. Look for small pockets that none of your remaining pieces can fill. Try saving long straight pieces for narrow passages, and compare a piece’s corners with the board’s inside and outside edges before placing it in a roomy central area.

Use Help to learn

Help me or H checks your current arrangement. It can highlight a safe next placement, or suggest removing a piece when the board has become tangled. The game can accept more than one valid solution. A hint is most useful when you study which space it preserves for your remaining pieces.

Play at your own pace

Moves and time help you compare attempts, but you can finish a level before worrying about stars or speed. Settings lets you control sound effects and music separately, reduce motion, and use fullscreen. Progress and best times are saved in this browser; they do not transfer through an online account.

Read the full Cozy Fit guide for the first-board example, star rules, hint limitations, and answers to common control problems. For a different kind of puzzle, try creating a photo puzzle to share.
