Guide Momo through open space, where every platform sweeps its own circle around a fixed point. These are solid — you can ride one all the way around and wait as long as you like. The trick is that you and your target are both moving: watch the two loops, wait until your platform swings near and the next one comes round to meet it, then jump.

HOW TO PLAY

About Broshere

Broshere (broshere.com) is a small, solo-built collection of privacy-first web utilities and mini-games.

The web utilities process your files, photos, or inputs directly inside your web browser using standard HTML5 features. There's no backend server or database involved in that processing, so nothing you open or edit is uploaded anywhere.

New tools and games are added whenever something seems worth building — there's no fixed schedule.

Advertising: This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Standard web logs: Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes. This isn't used to identify individual visitors personally.

Children's privacy: Broshere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
