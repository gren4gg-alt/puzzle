Guide Momo across swinging pendulums over the valley. Each platform hangs from an anchor and sweeps a slow arc — it lingers at the peaks and races through the bottom. Wait for two arcs to line up, then hop. Jumping off one while it's moving fast flings you further.

HOW TO PLAY

About Broshere

Broshere (broshere.com) is a small, solo-built collection of privacy-first web utilities and mini-games.

The web utilities process your files, photos, or inputs directly inside your web browser using standard HTML5 features. There's no backend server or database involved in that processing, so nothing you open or edit is uploaded anywhere.

New tools and games are added whenever something seems worth building — there's no fixed schedule.

Privacy Policy

What we don't collect: Broshere's tools (photo resizing, media compression, puzzle creation, and similar) run entirely inside your browser. Files, photos, or other data you process through them stay on your device and are never uploaded to, or stored on, any server we operate.

Advertising: This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and opt out of personalized advertising, at google.com/settings/ads or via the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page.

Standard web logs: Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes. This isn't used to identify individual visitors personally.

Children's privacy: Broshere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
