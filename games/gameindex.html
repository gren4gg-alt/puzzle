Five games, five ways to improve

Cozy Fit: think about the space left over

Drag shaped pieces into a silhouette without overlapping. Rotation unlocks in level 3, and Help checks whether your current arrangement can still be completed. Finish a board to open the next stop on a 50-level map.

Packing strategy, hints, and controls

Word Fall: build words under pressure

Choose individual tiles to form a word. Accurate submissions restore time, while longer words and quick chains increase the score. Start with Easy and All, then learn the scoring example before raising the difficulty.

Rules, scoring, and word strategy

ROLL: read the next hazard

Jump, double-jump, duck, and move as an endless course changes around you. Distance is your score. It uses a landscape playfield, so rotate a phone before you start.

Controls and jump timing

Drift Hop: wait for a landing

Guide Momo across moving islands to a portal. Eight levels introduce vertical drift, bounce pads, circular orbits, and pendulum arcs. A platform’s direction matters as much as its current position.

The complete eight-level guide

Dune Runner: choose a route

Steer through a 3D valley, collect shards, and maintain a scoring chain. Logs, walls, rolling boulders, and storm rocks each call for a different response. Adjust graphics and steering in the settings menu.

Hazards, chains, and settings

Make a short session useful

Choose one goal for the next run: submit fewer invalid words, save the second jump until needed, or observe one complete platform cycle. After a loss, identify the decision that led to it. Changing one thing gives you more useful feedback than trying every input faster.

Playing on another device

Check the guide for keyboard and touch controls before starting. Fullscreen can give you more room, while a lower graphics setting can help Dune Runner on a slower device. Saved records and progress stay in the current browser when supported; changing devices does not bring an online profile with you.

About the games

Broshere games are small, original browser games made for a quick break. They open directly in your browser, do not require an account, and save any game progress only on the device you are using when a game supports saved progress.

How to choose a game

Choose Cozy Fit for a spatial packing puzzle, Ball Runner for a fast reflex challenge, Word Fall for a word-building challenge, Drift Hop for carefully timed platform jumps, or Dune Runner for an endless run through a shifting desert. Each game has its own controls and difficulty curve, so use its opening screen or how-to-play view before starting.

Advertising and privacy

Broshere may show advertising to keep the games free. Advertising is separate from gameplay, and no account is required to play. Read the Privacy Policy for information about advertising technologies, cookies, and choices.
