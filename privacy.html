Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Broshere (broshere.com) handles information when you use this website.

Browser-based tools

Some Broshere tools are designed to process files, photos, or other input locally in your web browser. For those tools, the input is processed by the browser and is not uploaded to or stored on a Broshere server as part of the normal operation of the tool.

Because tools can work differently, please check the individual tool page for any tool-specific information about data processing.

Advertising

Broshere may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use cookies, device identifiers, IP address, browser information, and similar technologies to provide, measure, limit, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Where required, Broshere will present a consent message that lets visitors make choices about advertising cookies and personalized ads. Visitors can also review Google's advertising technologies and manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Third parties may place and read cookies in your browser or use web beacons and IP addresses as a result of advertising on the site. See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Game records and settings

Games may store scores, cleared levels, fall counts, in-game coins, wardrobe choices, and control settings in local browser storage. Cozy Fit also stores unlocked levels, best completion times, and sound, music, and reduced-motion preferences. Advertising is disabled in the current Cozy Fit web game. These records stay associated with the browser you use and are not synchronized through a Broshere account. You can remove them through your browser’s site-data controls, which can also remove saved progress.

Files you share or download

The photo tools and compressor use local browser processing. Downloading an output saves a new file on your device. If you share that file through email, messaging, or cloud storage, the chosen service handles that transfer under its own policies. A scrambled photo puzzle is not encryption and should not be used to protect sensitive images.

Model Workbench

Model Workbench processes selected 3D models and companion files in the current browser tab. Models are not uploaded to Broshere for conversion. Its queue and generated results are held in memory and are cleared on reload; download outputs to keep them. Compression settings and the selected profile are stored locally under model-workbench:v1. The sample model is generated in the browser. Libraries, decoder components, and fonts are requested from jsDelivr and Google Fonts, so these providers receive ordinary network request information. Missing external resources referenced by a model must be supplied with its files rather than fetched automatically.

Cookies and similar technologies

Broshere itself may use browser storage or similar technologies where needed for site functionality. Third-party services, including advertising providers, may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. You can control or delete cookies using your browser settings; doing so may affect how some services work.

Hosting and technical logs

Our hosting or infrastructure providers may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request information, and timestamps for security, reliability, and performance purposes.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact Broshere by email, the information you include in your message will be received by the site operator for the purpose of responding to you. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Children's privacy

Broshere is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party services

Pages may request third-party fonts, styles, scripts, or game libraries, including resources from Google Fonts and content delivery networks. These requests can expose technical information such as your IP address and browser details to the provider. Local image processing does not mean the entire webpage makes no network requests. Providers handle those requests according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, tools, or services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new last-updated date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to podugenerations@gmail.com.
