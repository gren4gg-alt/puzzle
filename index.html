Local processing, with clear limits

Your selected photos, media, and 3D models are processed by browser features on your device. The pages still use hosting and may load third-party scripts, fonts, and advertising resources. Games that remember records use local browser storage rather than an account. Read the Privacy Policy for the distinction, and keep original files before editing or sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Nothing on Broshere requires sign-up, login, or any personal information. Open a tool and start using it.

Are my files or photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool processes files locally in your browser using JavaScript. The tool does not upload the selected files for processing. Other page resources, such as fonts and advertising scripts, can still make network requests.

Is Broshere free to use?

Yes. Every tool and game on this site is free to use, with no subscriptions or paywalls.

Which browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge — on desktop or mobile. Since the tools rely on standard browser APIs, very old browser versions may not support some features, and a few games may not work on every device.

Can I suggest a new tool or report a bug?

Yes — email podugenerations@gmail.com with what you'd like to see or what went wrong, and it'll get read.
